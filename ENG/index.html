AI Activities

Interactive simulations and hands-on labs for learning about Artificial Intelligence.

AI search and problem solving

Search algorithms in mazes

Explore BFS, DFS, Greedy best-first and A* and see how they spread through the maze, square by square. Ch. 2 · Fundamental search algorithms(pp. 51–65)

Search algorithms in trees

Visualize fundamental search algorithms in a search tree and follow the search node by node. Ch. 2 · Fundamental search algorithms(pp. 51–65)

Five in a row in a partially observable environment

Play five in a row without seeing all the squares to experience partial observability. Ch. 2 · Environments(pp. 43–45)

Minimax with alpha‑beta pruning

Try the Minimax algorithm on a search tree. Follow the updates to the alpha-beta values during the search and see how alpha-beta pruning makes the search more efficient. Ch. 2 · Decision algorithms for games(pp. 66–74)

Decision trees

Build a decision tree that tells venomous snakes from harmless ones. Test it on snakes it has never seen and compare it with the tree an algorithm builds. Ch. 2 · Decision trees(p. 47) Ch. 3 · Decision trees(pp. 91–94)

Machine learning

Supervised learning

The Naïve Bayes lab

Train a Naive Bayes classifier to tell spam from ordinary email and see the mathematics behind the classification. Ch. 3 · Naive Bayes classifier(pp. 107–110)

Neural networks

The neural networks lab

Build, train and explore a neural network. Follow the computations in each node, then let the network make predictions based on the input. Ch. 3 · Neural networks(pp. 112–117)

Train a neural network

Follow a training round from beginning to end: forward pass, loss, backpropagation and weight updates.Advanced Ch. 3 · Neural networks(pp. 112–117)

Visualizing decision boundaries

Place data points and compare how linear models and neural networks draw the boundaries between classes, and why neural networks are often needed. Ch. 3 · Neural networks(pp. 112–117)

Computer vision with CNNs

Follow how an image-recognition model reads an image, from pixels through filters and feature maps to a classification. See how filters catch different patterns. CNN isn't covered in the textbook

Unsupervised learning

k‑means clustering

Explore how an algorithm finds patterns in data without knowing what it is looking for. Run it step by step and see how the choice of k shapes the grouping. Ch. 3 · The k‑means algorithm(pp. 120–122)

Reinforcement learning

Q‑learning simulation

Train an agent to navigate a grid using reinforcement learning. Follow how the agent learns the best path by trying different actions and updating its Q-values. Ch. 3 · Reinforcement learning(pp. 124–130)

NIM with reinforcement learning

Watch an AI system learn to play NIM from scratch. Train it over different numbers of games, follow the strategy forming in a Q-table and try playing against it. Ch. 3 · Reinforcement learning(pp. 124–130)

Language models

The language model lab

Build your own small language model from any text and see how it picks the next word, word by word. Ch. 4 · How a language model works(pp. 141–142)

AI and society

The bias lab

Train a recruitment AI on skewed historical data. Discover how discrimination arises and how hard it is to get rid of. Ch. 1 · Bias(pp. 31–32)

The AI timeline: When was that?

Test your knowledge of AI history. Place events on a timeline and score points. Choose to practice solo or challenge a friend in two-player mode. Ch. 1 · The history of AI(pp. 14–19)
