k-means clustering

Follow the algorithm step by step as it groups different kinds of datasets that come with no right answer.

  • 1Choose a dataset – study the points and guess how many groups there are.
  • 2Place centroids – decide on k and put out the centre points.
  • 3Assign data points – every point goes to the nearest centroid.
  • 4Move centroids – each centroid moves to the middle of its points, then steps 3 and 4 repeat.
  • 5Evaluate the choice of k – compare how different values of k affect the result.
